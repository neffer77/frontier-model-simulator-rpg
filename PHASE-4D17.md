# Phase 4D.17 — Workforce Planning, Span-of-Control Debt, Internal Mobility & Critical-Role Risk

Phase 4D.17 turns organization design into capacity and continuity planning.

## Workforce planning

The company now maintains target headcount, manager capacity, and a desired redundancy level for critical technical roles. Workforce shape is evaluated alongside the existing hiring and organization-management systems.

## Span-of-control debt

Teams without leadership and managers with excessive direct-report loads create span-of-control debt. That debt reduces effective execution capacity instead of being a purely visual org-chart issue.

Players can create team leads from internal talent, trading a small amount of short-term sentiment for better management coverage.

## Critical-role risk

The simulator evaluates four critical ownership areas: distributed training, inference serving, data provenance, and safety/evals. Each role looks for technically credible primary and backup owners.

A role with no qualified owner creates severe continuity risk. A role with only one qualified owner becomes a human single point of failure.

## Cross-training

Players can cross-train employees into critical domains. Cross-training increases the relevant skills, adds a small workload cost, and improves institutional memory. This creates a direct way to build redundancy before a departure or incident exposes the gap.

## Internal mobility

Employees can move between teams without leaving the company. Internal moves improve autonomy, promotion readiness, and employee sentiment while preserving institutional knowledge.

This gives the player another option besides external hiring when a team needs capacity or an employee needs a stronger career path.

## Integration

Phase 4D.17 builds on hiring, performance management, promotions, talent sentiment, organizational memory, and reorganization systems. Workforce risk feeds back into effective execution capacity.

Browser/mobile and Scriptable builds both load the new module.

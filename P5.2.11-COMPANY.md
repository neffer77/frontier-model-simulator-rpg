# P5.2.11 — Native FrontierOS Company

## Goal
Make Company a first-class FrontierOS executive workspace without reimplementing the Phase 4D simulation systems.

## Native ownership
`company.open` now owns the FrontierOS Company route. The native app exposes six views:

- **Overview** — runway, cash, valuation, board confidence, founder control, investor patience, executive risk and specialist-app handoffs.
- **Board** — quarterly operating plan, forecast, competitor response and board review.
- **Governance** — founder authority, board seats, formal motions, vetoes and fiduciary posture.
- **Restructuring** — macro shocks, term sheets, covenant pressure, restructuring, secondaries and acquisition offers.
- **Leadership** — executive factions, retention, culture, succession and CEO transition.
- **History** — combined board, governance, macro and leadership decision history.

Finance remains the canonical detailed capital-allocation app. Projects and People remain the canonical delivery and workforce apps. Company links to them instead of duplicating their workflows.

## Canonical simulation boundary
The existing Phase 4D systems remain mutation owners:

- `quarterly-board.js`
- `governance-board.js`
- `macro-restructuring.js`
- `executive-politics.js`
- `financing-strategy.js`

`company-adapter.js` provides one explicit compatibility boundary between older `state.quarterly` / `state.financing` records and current `state.quarterlyBoard.board` / `state.financeStrategy` records. Native Company actions always pass through `frontierCompanyDispatch`, emit action evidence, reconcile legacy writes back into current canonical state, and then re-render the native workspace.

## Deep links
Supported examples:

- `frontieros://company/overview`
- `frontieros://company/board`
- `frontieros://company/governance`
- `frontieros://company/restructuring`
- `frontieros://company/leadership`
- `frontieros://company/history`

## Evidence
`tests/company-frontieros.mjs` runs the native Company app through the real desktop and phone shells and verifies:

- command-owned launch
- six-view native surface
- quarterly plan mutation
- formal governance vote
- macro shock and reconciliation into canonical board valuation
- executive alignment history
- deep-link routing
- `company.*` telemetry
- zero page errors

Focused evidence is written to `artifacts/company-frontieros` and is release-blocking through the `frontieros-company` gate.

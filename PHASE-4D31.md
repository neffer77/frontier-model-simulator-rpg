# Phase 4D.31 — Security Governance, Red Teaming, Insider Risk & Model Theft

Phase 4D.31 turns security into a persistent operating tradeoff for a frontier-model company.

## Security posture

The simulator tracks four linked dimensions: overall security posture, red-team maturity, privileged-access risk, and model-asset protection. Security posture reflects both technical defenses and organizational conditions such as control maturity, employee overload, and attrition pressure.

## Security operating policy

Players can choose a fast, balanced, or locked-down operating model. A fast posture preserves execution speed but reduces model protection. Lockdown improves model protection while imposing a small execution-capacity cost.

## Red-team exercises

Tabletop and full red-team exercises cost cash but improve red-team maturity and model protection. Full exercises provide larger gains and represent realistic recurring investments rather than one-time permanent immunity.

## Insider and privileged-access risk

Privileged risk increases when employee workload and attrition pressure are high and when enterprise controls are weak. This connects security outcomes to talent management and governance rather than treating insider risk as random chance alone.

## Security incidents

The simulator can generate four major security scenarios:

- privileged insider exfiltration attempts
- frontier model-weight theft attempts
- compromised build/dependency incidents
- privileged credential compromise

Incident severity is reduced by existing security posture and operational-resilience maturity.

## Incident response

Players can choose quiet containment, forensic response, or emergency shutdown. Quiet containment is cheap but weaker and can damage trust. Forensic response costs more but improves defenses. Emergency shutdown delivers the strongest containment at the largest execution cost.

A sufficiently severe model-weight theft incident can damage customer trust and create additional legal/discovery burden.

## Model protection investment

Dedicated model-protection investment raises asset protection and reduces privileged-access risk, but costs capital and creates a small execution burden from stronger controls.

## Integration

Phase 4D.31 builds directly on employee workload and attrition, internal controls, enterprise risk, legal investigations, public communications, customer trust, and operational resilience.

Browser/mobile and Scriptable builds both load the security-governance module.

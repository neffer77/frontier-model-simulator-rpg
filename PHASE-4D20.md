# Phase 4D.20 — Program Management, Launch Trains, Scope Cuts & Schedule Recovery

Phase 4D.20 turns multiple projects into coordinated launch programs with shared deadlines and explicit readiness tradeoffs.

## Launch trains

Players can group several active portfolio projects into a launch train with one target day. A train rolls up project delivery, dependency blocking, staffing overload, and release-governance state into one program-level view.

## Readiness gates

Launch readiness is calculated across quality, schedule, people capacity, and reliability. Blocked projects, late critical-path forecasts, overloaded engineers, incomplete release gates, and reliability freezes all reduce readiness.

A train cannot launch below the readiness threshold, so the player must actively resolve the underlying program risk rather than clicking through it.

## Scope and schedule recovery

When a launch slips, the player can choose among four recovery approaches:

- Cut scope: recover schedule quickly by reducing delivered scope and strategic value.
- Surge staffing: improve schedule at the cost of additional team load.
- Harden quality: spend more time to improve quality and customer confidence.
- Move the launch date: create schedule margin while accepting a small trust cost.

These options intentionally trade date, scope, quality, and people health against one another.

## Coordinated launch outcomes

Successful launch trains improve customer trust and convert some project strategic value into modeled revenue. Higher-readiness launches create better customer outcomes than barely-ready launches.

## Integration

Phase 4D.20 builds directly on project staffing, dependency graphs, critical-path forecasting, workforce capacity, release governance, and customer trust/economics.

Browser/mobile and Scriptable builds both load the program-management module.

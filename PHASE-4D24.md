# Phase 4D.24 — Competitive Intelligence, Rival Labs, Market Signals & Technology-Race Dynamics

Phase 4D.24 turns competition into a persistent strategic system rather than isolated scripted events.

## Persistent rival labs

The simulator now tracks three rival organizations with different strategic profiles: a frontier-capability lab, an efficiency-focused systems lab, and an enterprise-focused AI company.

Each rival has evolving capability, efficiency, enterprise strength, talent pull, and compute position. Their strongest strategic dimension improves over time, so the competitive landscape keeps moving even when the player does nothing.

## Competitive positioning

The player's lab receives a comparable position across frontier capability, efficiency, enterprise strength, talent, and compute. These values are derived from existing model progress, execution capacity, customer trust, recruiting reputation, and compute investment.

The game compares your position against the strongest rival and calculates a persistent technology-race pressure score.

## Market signals

Players can collect competitive signals such as benchmark leaks, inference price cuts, enterprise wins, hiring waves, and large compute reservations.

Signals have confidence levels and noise. A perceived signal is therefore not guaranteed to represent the rival's true underlying position.

## Intelligence confidence

The simulator aggregates recent signal quality into an intelligence-confidence score. Better intelligence makes it easier to justify waiting or differentiating instead of reflexively chasing every external announcement.

## Strategic responses

For each rival, the player can choose among five responses:

- race the frontier
- differentiate strategically
- recruit harder
- secure additional compute
- hold position and gather more evidence

Each response has real opportunity costs across portfolio strategy, compensation pressure, cash, execution capacity, customer trust, or option discipline.

## Technology-race consequences

High race pressure contributes to executive pressure and can make aggressive frontier reactions increasingly tempting. The system therefore connects competition to the existing roadmap, organization, talent, financing, and portfolio mechanics.

## Integration

Phase 4D.24 builds on investment committee decisions, portfolio themes, employee talent systems, customer trust, execution capacity, compute access, and executive pressure.

Browser/mobile and Scriptable builds both load the competitive-intelligence module.

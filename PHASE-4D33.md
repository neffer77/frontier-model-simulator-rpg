# Phase 4D.33 — CEO/Executive Endgame, Succession, IPO/Acquisition & Long-Term Outcomes

Phase 4D.33 adds a true company endgame built from the institution the player created.

## Endgame readiness

The simulator calculates separate readiness for an IPO, strategic acquisition, and continued independence. These scores combine governance confidence, public/customer trust, enterprise risk, program maturity, ecosystem/platform power, cash strength, safety posture, security posture, and succession depth.

No exit path is automatically available just because the company is valuable.

## Succession

The player can designate a named NPC employee as successor. Candidates are ranked from existing promotion readiness and trust rather than created as abstract executive tokens.

Preparing a successor improves promotion readiness and trust, making leadership continuity an investment that must happen before the founder leaves.

## IPO

IPO preparation consumes cash and requires a sufficiently mature institution. Public-company readiness rewards governance, controls, safety, operating maturity, trust, and financial strength.

## Acquisition

Strategic acquisition readiness values platform position, safety/security strength, organizational maturity, trust, cash position, governance, and succession depth.

## Independence

Remaining independent is also an explicit endgame path rather than the absence of an exit. It rewards operating maturity, safety, security, enterprise risk management, financial durability, ecosystem strength, and leadership depth.

## Leadership transition

The CEO can hand the company to the designated successor once that employee is sufficiently ready. Succession is therefore a fourth endgame outcome, not just preparation for another exit.

## Outcome quality and legacy

Committing to a viable path produces a fragile, strong, or landmark outcome based on readiness at the moment of commitment.

A persistent legacy score summarizes how durable the company became. It incorporates exit readiness, institutional independence, succession depth, and trust.

## Integration

Phase 4D.33 pulls directly from the systems built throughout Phase 4D, especially governance, public communications, enterprise risk, program learning, ecosystem power, security governance, and AI safety governance.

Browser/mobile integration is included.

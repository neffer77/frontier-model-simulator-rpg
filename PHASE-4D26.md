# Phase 4D.26 — Policy & Regulatory Strategy, Jurisdictions, Export Controls, Model-Access Rules & Government Relationships

Phase 4D.26 adds a fictional-but-realistic policy layer without encoding current real-world law as static game rules.

## Jurisdictions

The company can operate across multiple fictional regulatory blocs with different compliance burden, export pressure, model-access expectations, market value, and trust benefits.

Entering more jurisdictions increases possible market access, but low preparedness or weak relationships can raise regulatory risk.

## Model-access strategy

Players choose among broad API access, tiered access, and restricted frontier access. Broader access favors growth but attracts more scrutiny; tighter access sacrifices some growth in exchange for institutional trust and lower policy risk.

## Compliance investment

Players can invest directly in jurisdiction-specific compliance readiness. This costs cash and slightly consumes execution capacity, but reduces regulatory risk and improves durable market access.

## Government relationships

Government engagement builds relationship strength and company-wide policy capital. Better relationships modestly reduce regulatory risk and improve trust, but engagement consumes cash and creates an explicit strategic commitment.

## Localization

Localization spends more capital to improve preparedness, government relationships, and enterprise adoption in a jurisdiction. It is useful when the company wants durable access rather than simply entering a market.

## Export-control posture

The simulator exposes three abstract export strategies: conservative controls, a licensing-oriented approach, or aggressively pushing market access. These choices trade developer reach and growth against government trust and regulatory exposure.

## Persistent outcomes

The policy system calculates market access, regulatory risk, government trust, and export exposure. These feed back into enterprise trust, recurring revenue growth, valuation, ecosystem adoption, and organizational execution capacity.

## Integration

Phase 4D.26 builds on ecosystem strategy, competitive pressure, financing valuation, customer trust, company cash, and organization-management systems.

Browser/mobile and Scriptable builds both load the new policy module.

# Phase 4D.15 — Employee Sentiment, Talent Markets & Organizational Memory

Phase 4D.15 extends executive culture into the broader employee population. Headcount is no longer interchangeable: morale, external talent pressure, recruiting reputation, compensation pressure, departures, and knowledge continuity now affect the company over time.

## Employee sentiment

Company-wide employee sentiment responds to organizational trust, executive loyalty, paging fatigue, and external talent-market pressure. Strong culture and leadership improve morale; overloaded teams and a hot labor market increase attrition pressure.

## Talent markets and recruiting reputation

The external talent market becomes more competitive during AI demand booms, runway crises, and high frontier ambition. Recruiting reputation changes with employee sentiment and reliability discipline. Poor reputation raises compensation pressure and makes retention more expensive.

## Attrition and knowledge loss

Talent departures can now remove institutional knowledge rather than merely reduce headcount. The severity of knowledge loss depends on how much organizational memory has already been captured.

Knowledge loss reduces resilience and board confidence, creating a long-term cost for churn-heavy strategies.

## Institutional memory

Institutional memory represents documentation, handoffs, runbooks, historical context, and tacit technical knowledge. Players can invest directly in knowledge systems to make the company more resilient to departures and leadership transitions.

## Alumni networks

Departed employees can still remain useful. Alumni-network investment improves referrals, recruiting reputation, and the ability to retain access to historical expertise. Departures also incrementally strengthen the alumni network instead of making every relationship disappear.

## People investments

Players can choose among:

- retention programs to improve sentiment and reduce compensation pressure
- knowledge-system investments to preserve technical continuity
- alumni-network investment to strengthen recruiting and external institutional memory

These compete directly with the same company cash used for compute, reliability, financing obligations, and roadmap delivery.

## Integration

Phase 4D.15 builds on executive culture, governance, quarterly planning, macro conditions, SLO/paging fatigue, and financing state. Browser/mobile and Scriptable WebView builds both load the new people-systems module.

# Phase 4D.30 — Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, Vendor Concentration & Operational Resilience

Phase 4D.30 makes operational resilience a first-class company system.

## Vendor concentration

The simulator now tracks concentration across cloud infrastructure, accelerator supply, identity services, and data-pipeline vendors. High concentration creates continuity risk even when normal operations are healthy.

Players can fund secondary providers. Redundancy costs cash but lowers concentration and improves business-continuity maturity.

## Business continuity and disaster recovery

The company now has separate BCP and DR maturity scores. Players can invest directly in recovery capability, accepting a small near-term execution cost in exchange for lower outage impact and faster recovery.

## Resilience exercises

Tabletop exercises are inexpensive and create modest learning. Full exercises cost more but improve both BCP and DR maturity substantially. Exercises also add institutional memory.

## Supply-chain and service shocks

The simulator can generate four operational shocks:

- primary cloud regional outage
- accelerator supply interruption
- identity-provider disruption
- data-pipeline vendor failure

The realized severity of each shock depends on redundancy, BCP maturity, and DR maturity.

## Recovery

Active continuity incidents reduce effective execution capacity and can increase communications pressure. Recovery actions advance restoration based on DR maturity and whether a secondary provider exists.

Resolved incidents become organizational learning rather than disappearing without consequence.

## Enterprise risk integration

Continuity risk feeds into the Phase 4D.29 enterprise-risk system, so concentration and poor recovery readiness can raise board-level risk even before a severe outage occurs.

## Integration

Phase 4D.30 builds on enterprise risk, institutional memory, execution capacity, customer trust, and public communications.

Browser/mobile and Scriptable builds both load the operational-resilience module.

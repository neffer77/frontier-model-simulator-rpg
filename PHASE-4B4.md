# Phase 4B.4 — Hiring + Org Design

Phase 4B.4 turns the persistent NPC team into a growing engineering organization with recruiting, compensation, team structure, management, rival pressure, and attrition risk.

## Implemented

- Persistent candidate pipeline with six frontier-AI engineering candidates.
- Structured interviews scoring technical depth, collaboration, and judgment.
- Standard and stronger compensation offers with acceptance logic.
- Persistent headcount and annual compensation budgets.
- New hires become full NPC employees and inherit the career/mentoring systems.
- Eight functional org teams: Research, Pretraining, Data, Post-Training, Inference, Evals, Safety, and Infrastructure.
- Persistent team membership and team-lead assignments.
- Org-chart UI showing team size, leads, levels, specialties, and attrition risk.
- Rival recruiting pressure that can trigger external offers for vulnerable employees.
- Retention choices: compensation counter, increased ownership, or allow departure.
- Attrition risk derived from workload, autonomy, trust, compensation, and rival pressure.
- Responsive browser/mobile UI.
- Scriptable WebView integration.

## Design principle

Hiring should create technical and organizational tradeoffs rather than functioning as a generic stat shop. Candidates differ by specialization, cost, and working style; organizational health depends on role fit, workload, autonomy, compensation, and leadership structure.

## Next increments

1. Interview rounds with role-specific technical questions and evidence-based candidate evaluation.
2. Open requisitions tied to actual capability gaps in the org.
3. Headcount planning tied to company cash flow and model-roadmap commitments.
4. Managers affecting mentoring bandwidth, psychological safety, and team execution.
5. Cross-team project staffing and model-specific ownership matrices.
6. More nuanced attrition and counteroffer decisions based on employee ambitions.
7. Rival labs actively recruiting specific specialties during technology races.
8. Hiring-market events that change candidate supply and compensation expectations.

## Next major phase

Phase 4C shifts back toward maximum technical realism: editable training configs, realistic logs and filesystem/terminal workflows, TensorBoard-style metrics, GPU fleet visualization, NCCL traces, profiler timelines, data-pipeline inspection, and small code/config debugging exercises.

# Phase 4D.28 — Litigation, Investigations, Discovery Burden, Legal Holds, Settlement Strategy & Whistleblower Cases

Phase 4D.28 turns legal exposure into an operating constraint rather than a one-off penalty.

## Active legal matters

The company can now face regulatory investigations, enterprise contract disputes, model/IP disputes, and whistleblower complaints. Each matter has severity, discovery burden, financial exposure, investigation progress, and a persistent lifecycle.

## Discovery burden

Active matters generate discovery workload. Discovery burden lowers effective execution capacity, representing engineers, executives, and operations staff being pulled into document collection, interviews, preservation, and legal review.

Players can invest in a dedicated discovery response to move a matter forward and reduce its discovery complexity at a direct cash cost.

## Legal holds

A company-wide legal hold can be issued to improve preservation coverage. This costs money and adds operating overhead, but it reduces preservation-related legal risk as matters progress.

Releasing the hold reduces overhead but weakens preservation coverage.

## Settlement, cooperation, and litigation posture

For each active matter, the player can cooperate, fight, or settle.

- Cooperation speeds resolution, reduces severity, and can improve government trust.
- Fighting preserves the dispute but increases legal spend and can raise public-risk pressure.
- Settlement closes the matter immediately at a cash and trust cost tied to the matter's exposure and severity.

## Whistleblower cases

Whistleblower matters track reporter credibility. Highly credible reports increase public and employee pressure if they remain unresolved.

Players can explicitly protect the reporter, which improves employee sentiment and public trust while reducing case severity.

## Legal risk and downstream consequences

The simulator aggregates active-case severity, whistleblower credibility, and discovery complexity into legal risk and discovery burden. These metrics feed into execution capacity and board confidence, while individual case decisions also affect cash, public trust, government trust, and employee sentiment.

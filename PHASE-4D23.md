# Phase 4D.23 — Scenario Planning, Option Value, Staged Funding Gates & NPC Investment Committee Debates

Phase 4D.23 makes uncertainty itself a portfolio-management mechanic.

## Scenario planning

The investment committee maintains probabilities for three operating scenarios: a capability boom, measured expansion, and a capital/reliability squeeze. Each scenario values scientific, platform, commercial, and reliability initiatives differently.

Changing scenario probabilities therefore changes the risk-adjusted value of the existing initiative portfolio rather than merely changing descriptive text.

## Scenario expected value

Every initiative receives a scenario-weighted expected value. The calculation combines its upside, cost, risk, strategic category, and the player's current probability distribution across scenarios.

This allows initiatives to be attractive in one future and weak in another.

## Option value

The simulator separately estimates option value. High-uncertainty initiatives can retain value because a small staged investment preserves the ability to expand later if evidence improves.

Option value declines as more funding gates are consumed, representing the gradual transition from reversible exploration to committed execution.

## Staged funding gates

Initiatives can now be funded through three evidence gates rather than requiring the full investment immediately. Each gate consumes a tranche of cash, produces evidence, advances progress, and reduces uncertainty.

Players can also hold a gate to gather modest additional evidence or stop the initiative entirely.

## NPC investment committee

Named NPC employees participate in investment-committee debates. Their technical profile, autonomy, and operating orientation influence whether they recommend funding, holding, or stopping an initiative.

The committee records individual stances, its majority recommendation, scenario EV, and option value. The player still makes the final decision.

## Option discipline

The company receives an option-discipline score based on how consistently uncertain initiatives use staged gates and on accumulated program maturity. This represents the organization's ability to learn before making irreversible commitments.

## Integration

Phase 4D.23 builds directly on portfolio strategy, initiative uncertainty, cash, NPC skills and relationships, program maturity, and organizational learning.

Browser/mobile and Scriptable builds both load the investment-committee module.

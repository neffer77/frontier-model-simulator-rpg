# Phase 4D.14 — Executive-Team Politics, Succession & Organizational Culture

Phase 4D.14 turns senior leadership into a persistent political system rather than a set of static NPCs.

## Executive factions

The company now begins with four executive archetypes: CTO/frontier, COO/operators, CRO/commercial, and Chief Safety Officer/reliability. Each tracks loyalty, ambition, retention, succession readiness, allies, rivals, and faction alignment.

Executive pressure responds to company reality. Frontier leaders become restless when roadmap pressure rises, reliability leaders react to freezes and reliability stress, commercial leaders respond to weak investor patience, and all executives become less secure when board confidence or customer trust deteriorates.

## Leadership actions

Players can empower, mediate with, or challenge executives. Empowerment improves loyalty, retention, and succession readiness but marginally reduces founder authority. Challenging leaders can strengthen founder authority while increasing departure risk. Mediation improves trust and board confidence.

## Alliances and conflict

The player can broker alliances or escalate factional conflict. Alliances improve loyalty and organizational trust. Rivalries reduce executive retention and board confidence and can turn otherwise manageable operating pressure into senior-leadership instability.

## Succession planning

Any active executive can be nominated as the CEO successor. Nomination improves readiness and board confidence. A sufficiently prepared successor can later take the CEO role, permanently changing the governance system and company culture while reducing founder operating authority.

## Retention risk

Executives with weak retention scores can leave the company. Departures lower board confidence and organizational trust and can invalidate the current succession plan.

## Culture

Culture now tracks frontier ambition, execution discipline, commercial orientation, reliability discipline, and organizational trust. These values drift over simulated days based on fiduciary posture, release freezes, executive pressure, board confidence, and leadership choices.

Culture is intentionally path dependent: repeated choices gradually define what kind of frontier-model company the player has built.

## Integration

Phase 4D.14 builds on the governance, financing, roadmap, reliability, and board systems. Leadership choices alter founder authority, board confidence, and company culture rather than existing in isolation.

Browser/mobile and Scriptable WebView builds both load the new module.

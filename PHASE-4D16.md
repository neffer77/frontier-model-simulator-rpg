# Phase 4D.16 — Compensation Bands, Hiring Funnels, Promotions, Performance Management & Reorganizations

Phase 4D.16 turns hiring and headcount into a deeper organizational operating system. Employees now exist inside explicit compensation bands and career structures, while hiring funnels, performance calibration, promotion capacity, and reorganizations interact with sentiment, memory, attrition, and execution.

## Compensation bands

Each engineering level has a minimum, midpoint, and maximum compensation band. Employees can sit low or high inside their band, and market adjustments can move them toward competitive compensation when external pressure rises.

Compensation remains tied to the existing hiring budget and attrition systems rather than acting as a cosmetic stat.

## Hiring funnel

The recruiting pipeline now models sourced candidates, screens, onsites, offers, and accepted offers. Conversion depends on recruiting reputation, alumni strength, and market compensation pressure from Phase 4D.15.

## Performance calibration

Performance cycles rate employees across needs improvement, meets expectations, strong, and exceptional. Ratings combine technical capability, trust, workload, and demonstrated contribution.

Strong performance builds promotion readiness. Poor performance can reduce readiness and feed into coaching or structured improvement decisions.

## Promotions and career tracks

Promotions consume a limited promotion budget and require sufficient readiness. Promotion increases level and compensation while improving trust.

Employees can also move between IC and manager tracks. Manager transitions trade individual-contributor focus for organizational leverage and autonomy.

## Performance actions

Leaders can coach an employee, start a structured improvement plan, or change their IC/manager track. These actions affect trust, attrition risk, morale, and career progression.

## Reorganizations

Three initial reorg patterns are available:

- Flatten management to reduce layers and improve decision speed at a morale cost.
- Centralize platform engineering to improve execution capacity while risking institutional-memory disruption.
- Mission realignment to shift talent toward safety and mission priorities at a small velocity cost.

Reorganizations are persistent history events rather than free instantaneous optimizations.

## Integration

The system builds on Phase 4D.15 employee sentiment and organizational memory and the earlier Phase 4B.4 hiring/org model. Compensation affects attrition risk, recruiting reputation affects funnel conversion, and reorgs alter sentiment, memory, and execution capacity.

Browser/mobile and Scriptable WebView builds both load the module.

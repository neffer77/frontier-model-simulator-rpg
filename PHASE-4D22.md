# Phase 4D.22 — Portfolio Strategy, Initiative Proposals, Resource Bidding & Kill/Continue Decisions

Phase 4D.22 adds explicit investment governance above the project and program layers.

## Strategic themes

Players can choose a portfolio theme: frontier capability, enterprise durability, efficient scale, or balanced portfolio. The selected theme changes how initiative value is weighted, so the same proposal can look attractive or weak depending on company strategy.

## Initiative proposals

The simulator includes proposals for next-generation architecture research, training-platform modernization, enterprise controls, and reliability hardening. Each initiative has a capital requirement, people requirement, upside estimate, and uncertainty level.

## Risk-adjusted expected value

Initiatives are scored using expected upside, risk, cost, strategic-theme fit, and current program maturity. This makes proposals comparable without pretending that their nominal upside is guaranteed.

## Finite capital and people budgets

Funding an initiative consumes both portfolio capital and a people allocation. The player cannot approve every proposal at once. Funded initiatives therefore compete with one another for scarce organizational capacity.

## Portfolio review decisions

Funded initiatives can be continued, pivoted, or killed.

Continuing improves progress and gradually reduces uncertainty. Pivoting gives up some progress and upside while reducing risk and slightly improving organizational learning. Killing returns people and future capital capacity but can slightly hurt sentiment.

This creates an explicit mechanism for stopping weak investments instead of letting sunk-cost projects continue indefinitely.

## Initiative completion

Initiatives accumulate progress as the portfolio operates. Completed investments release their people allocation, produce a risk-adjusted realized benefit, and modestly improve organizational execution capacity.

## Integration

Phase 4D.22 builds on company cash, program maturity, organizational execution capacity, customer revenue, employee sentiment, and the earlier project/program management systems.

Browser/mobile and Scriptable builds both load the new portfolio-strategy module.

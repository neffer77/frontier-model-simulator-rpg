# Phase 4D.21 — Launch Retrospectives, Program Learning, Planning Accuracy & PM/NPC Ownership

Phase 4D.21 closes the loop between planning, launching, and organizational learning.

## Named program ownership

Each launch train can now be assigned to a specific NPC employee. Program ownership increases autonomy and promotion readiness, turning delivery coordination into a career-development path rather than an abstract player-only responsibility.

Owners can be coached to improve their program-management capability and trust.

## Planning accuracy

Completed launch trains receive a planning-accuracy score based on the difference between planned and actual duration, the number of recovery actions required, and launch readiness.

A launch that lands on time only because of repeated emergency recovery therefore scores worse than a genuinely well-planned program.

## Launch retrospectives

After a train launches, the player can run a retrospective. The retrospective records planning accuracy, recovery count, readiness, program owner, and a concrete lesson based on the outcome.

Retrospectives improve institutional memory and give the responsible NPC additional learning and promotion readiness.

## Program maturity

The simulator now calculates program maturity from three dimensions:

- historical planning accuracy
- percentage of programs with named owners
- retrospective coverage of completed launches

Higher program maturity modestly reduces program risk, representing better estimation, clearer accountability, and repeated organizational learning.

## Persistent lessons

Lessons from completed launch trains remain in a program-learning record. This gives the company a history of what worked and what repeatedly required schedule recovery.

## Integration

Phase 4D.21 builds directly on launch trains, recovery actions, NPC career progression, employee trust/autonomy, institutional memory, and program risk.

Browser/mobile and Scriptable builds both load the new program-learning module.
